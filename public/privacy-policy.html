Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

PatternGuru is a free, static reference for the Gang of Four software design patterns. There are no user accounts, no login, and no server-side database. This policy explains exactly what happens to data when you use the site.

1. Data we store on your own device

Everything you create on PatternGuru stays in your browser. We never transmit it anywhere, and we cannot read it. Specifically, the site writes to localStorage and IndexedDB to remember:

  • Your theme choice (light, dark, or high contrast) and preferred code language.
  • Patterns you mark as favourites, and your recently-viewed list.
  • Star ratings you give a pattern, and your reading-position per pattern.
  • Personal notes you type into the notes panel on any pattern page.
  • Reader settings and whether you have finished the guided tour.

Clearing your browser's site data for this domain permanently deletes all of it. Because it is device-local, it does not follow you to another browser or computer.

2. Microphone and voice navigation

PatternGuru offers optional voice commands. This uses the Web Speech API built into your browser, and it is off until you explicitly press the microphone button. Your browser will ask for microphone permission first. Audio is processed by your browser and its speech provider — PatternGuru does not record, store, or transmit audio. Do not enable the feature if you would rather not use it; every part of the site works without it.

3. Cookies and third-party advertising

PatternGuru itself sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics. The site is funded by advertising, and we use Google AdSense to display ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • Full detail is in Google's privacy & terms for partner sites.

4. Other third-party requests

Loading a page also requests web fonts from Google Fonts and, on some pages, preview images from Unsplash. Those providers see your IP address and user agent, as with any embedded resource. Static hosting is provided by Netlify, which keeps standard server access logs.

5. Children's privacy

PatternGuru is a technical reference aimed at software developers and students. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from them.

6. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to export or delete on your behalf. You control all stored data directly through your browser settings. If you are in the EEA or UK, note that the legal basis for advertising cookies is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the links in section 3.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it. Material changes will be noted on this page.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the contact page.
